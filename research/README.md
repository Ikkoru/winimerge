# Research: comparing images of different resolution in WinMerge / WinIMerge

Session of 2026-10-14. Read in this order:

1. `REPORT.md`: the study (question, method, results, recommendation, what was not tested).
2. `DISCOVERIES.md`: everything learned along the way about resampling filters, FreeImage, Pillow,
   OpenCV, libvips, the metrics and tools, WinMerge's viewing conditions. Each entry says whether it was
   measured, read in source/docs, or inferred.
3. `HANDOFF.md`: branches, draft PRs, how to build and test on Windows, what to do next upstream.
